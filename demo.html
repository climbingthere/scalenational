Who We Are

Built on Trust, Backed by Results

Family-Owned.
Atlanta-Built.

Summit Roofing Co. was founded right here in Atlanta with a simple mission: deliver honest, high-quality roofing work at a fair price. For over 15 years, we've served homeowners across metro Atlanta — from Buckhead to Decatur, Marietta to Roswell.

We're not a franchise or a storm-chasing crew. We're your neighbors. Every project is overseen by our owner, and we treat your home like it's our own. That's why more than 80% of our business comes from referrals.
