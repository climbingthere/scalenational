What We Do

Your team learns it by doing it, in your account

Your team's hands are on the keyboard. I sit with them on live campaigns and show them exactly what to do. Four phases, all inside your Business Manager.

01 · Build

The account and the tracking, set up right

We start in your Business Manager, on a screen share. Your person builds the account architecture while I direct every step: campaign structure, naming, and budget logic that stays readable as spend grows. Your team runs it, I keep it sharp

Launch isn't the finish line. Weekly working sessions on live campaigns: your person proposes the budget moves, creative refreshes, and the kill or scale calls, and I confirm or correct them before anything goes in. They build real judgment on your data, while the ads keep producing.

Questions I get on the first call

Straight answers, before you book.

Do you run the ads or just teach us? +
I teach by working alongside you on your live campaigns. You run them, I make sure you run them right. Your team makes every change in your own ad account with me in the session, guiding and course-correcting in real time, so the skill actually sticks. Nothing here is a recorded course, and nothing gets done behind your back.
What if we don't have an in-house person yet? +
That's fine, and it's common. We start the working sessions with whoever will touch the account for now, often that's the founder, and I'll help you write the role and screen candidates for real signal when you hire. A sharp marketer with no ads experience often beats a self-taught expert with bad habits. Building the skill is my job.
Who owns the ad account? +
You do, from day one. Everything gets built in your Business Manager: the account, pixel, Conversions API, audiences, and creative. If we stop working together tomorrow, you keep all of it.
What do you need from us? +
Three things. A real ad budget you control. Someone whose job this will be, or the intent to hire them. And a few hours a week for working sessions. If any of those are missing, the first call will surface it.
How is it priced? +
A flat monthly fee, month to month, with no long-term contract. Never a percentage of your ad spend, so I have no reason to push your budget up. The exact number depends on scope, and you'll have it in writing after the first call.
How long until our team runs it without you? +
It depends on your team's reps and your spend. The foundation is live within the first few weeks: account, tracking, first campaigns. Real independence takes months of reps on live campaigns, not days, and anyone promising mastery in two weeks is selling a course. What I can promise is that my role shrinks every month.
Who is this wrong for? +
If you can't fund a consistent ad budget, want to stay completely hands-off forever, or are shopping for someone to blame when a campaign dips, we're not a fit. I'd rather tell you that on the call than sign a bad engagement.
